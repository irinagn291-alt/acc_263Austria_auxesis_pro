Auxesis is a daily breath canvas. A ring is one breath round. Seven rings seal the day.

• Hold the circle, then swipe a span so today's rings can grow.
• Start round files the next ring. Long press sets your own pace.
• Swipe a span between one and ten minutes. Release to begin Inhale, Hold, and Exhale.
• End round stops a cycle that is already underway.
• Rings only grow. Each swipe must last as long as the ring outside it.
• Seven rings seal the day. After the seventh, the canvas stays read only.
• Practices lists sealed and partial days, each ring span, and a Day total.
• Stats shows Sealed days, Filed rings, Longest span, Consecutive days, and Rings this month.
• Settings includes Contact Auxesis, How rings ascend, Replay onboarding, Reset all data, and Haptics.

Rings stay on this device.
